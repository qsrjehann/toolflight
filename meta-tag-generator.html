About the Meta Tag Generator

    
Getting meta tags right across title, description, canonical, Open Graph, and Twitter Card all at once is easy to get wrong by hand. Meta Tag Generator builds all of them together with a live preview of the exact HTML you'll paste into your page.


    
Features

    
  • Title, description, keywords, canonical, author, and robots directive
  • Open Graph tags for social link previews
  • Twitter Card tags with summary or large-image layout
  • Live HTML preview, copy or download in one click


    
How to Use

    
Fill in your title, description, and other fields — the Open Graph and Twitter Card tags reuse your title/description automatically. Copy the generated HTML or download it, then paste it into your page's <head>.


    
Benefits

    
  • One tool covers every major meta tag instead of piecing them together from memory
  • Live preview catches typos before they go live
  • Copy or download the exact HTML snippet to paste into your page


    
What each tag does
  • Title: the clickable headline in search results. Keep it under about 60 characters so it is not cut off.
  • Meta description: the summary under the title. Around 120 to 160 characters works well; it does not directly rank pages but influences clicks.
  • Canonical: tells search engines which URL is the main version of a page, which prevents duplicate-content confusion.
  • Robots: directives such as index/noindex and follow/nofollow control how a page is treated.
  • Open Graph and Twitter Card: control the title, description and image shown when your link is shared on social platforms and messaging apps.
Common mistakes to avoid
Do not copy the same title and description across many pages; each page needs its own. Make the canonical an absolute URL that matches the address you want indexed, and do not canonicalise a page to a different page unless you really mean it. Be careful with noindex: leaving it on a live page removes it from search results. Social images are best at about 1200 × 630 pixels. The old keywords tag is ignored by major search engines, so do not spend time on it.
How to use the output
Fill in the fields, check the live preview, then copy or download the HTML and paste it inside the <head> section of your page. After publishing, check the page with a search-console inspection tool and a social share preview. Pair this with the Robots.txt Generator to control crawling, and the Keyword Density Checker to review the text itself. Planning content ideas? Try the Keyword Generator, and use the Word Counter to check title and description lengths. Everything is generated locally in your browser.

    
Frequently Asked Questions

    
Do I need to fill in every field?
No — only the fields you fill in appear in the generated output; empty fields are simply omitted.

    
Where do these tags go in my page?
All of the generated tags belong inside your HTML page's <head> section.
