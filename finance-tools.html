About ToolFlight's Finance Tools

These tools handle the money-math questions that come up before a real decision: what a loan actually costs per month (EMI), how much tax to add or remove (GST/VAT), what a discount really saves, how savings compound over years, where your income sits relative to others, and what an amount is worth in another currency. Each shows its formula next to the result so you can see how the number was reached, not just the number itself.

Money tools at a glance

  • Plan a loan: the EMI / Loan Calculator shows monthly payment, total interest and total cost.
  • Grow savings: the Compound Interest Calculator shows the effect of rate, time and contributions.
  • Handle tax: the GST / VAT Calculator adds or removes tax from any amount.
  • Compare prices: the Discount Calculator and Currency Converter.
  • Benchmark income: the Income Percentile Calculator.
  • Bill customers: the Invoice Maker creates professional invoices.

Good habits

Compare offers using the total cost, not only the headline rate or monthly payment, and include fees. Keep a record of the assumptions behind every calculation. Tax rates and rules change by country, so verify them with an official source or accountant.

Not financial advice

These tools provide estimates for planning and comparison. They do not replace advice from a licensed professional, and they run privately in your browser without uploading your figures.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these tools a substitute for financial advice?
No. They compute standard formulas (EMI, compound interest, GST, etc.) for quick estimates. For a real loan, investment, or tax filing, confirm the numbers with your lender, bank, or a licensed advisor.

How current are the currency conversion rates?
Currency Converter fetches live exchange rates at the time you use it, so figures reflect current market rates rather than a fixed table.

Is my financial data stored anywhere?
No. Figures you enter (loan amounts, income, prices) are calculated in your browser and are not saved or transmitted.
