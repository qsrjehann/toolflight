Frequently Asked Questions

Is my business data private?
Yes -- your business, customers, products, and invoices are only visible to you and anyone you've explicitly invited to your team.
Can I invite my team?
Yes -- open Team Members from the profile menu to invite people and set their role and permissions.
What happened to Suppliers, Expenses, Reports, and Accounting?
They're on the roadmap and shown in the sidebar so you know where they'll live, but they aren't built yet -- opening one shows an honest "coming soon" screen rather than fake data.

About the Invoice Maker

A free, no-signup way to create a professional invoice for a client or customer. Add your business details, the customer you're billing, and as many line items as the job needs — the subtotal, tax, discount, and total are calculated live as you type. When you're done, print the invoice directly or download it as a PDF. Everything happens in your browser; nothing is uploaded to a server, and your invoice isn't saved anywhere once you leave the page, so there's nothing to sign up for or clean up afterward.

Who this is for

Freelancers, small business owners, and anyone who needs to send a one-off invoice without setting up accounting software. If you find yourself creating invoices regularly and want to save your business details, customers, and past invoices for next time, a free account option is coming in a future update.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account?
No — Quick Invoice works entirely without signing up. An optional free account for saving your business and customer details is planned for a future update.
Is my invoice data saved anywhere?
No — everything you enter stays in your browser for this session only. Download or print your invoice before leaving the page, since nothing is saved automatically.
What currencies are supported?
USD, EUR, GBP, INR, CAD, AUD, JPY, AED, and PKR are available now, with the correct symbol shown on your invoice.
