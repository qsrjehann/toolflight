About the Rotate & Flip Tool

Fix a sideways photo or create a mirrored version in one click. Rotate & Flip Tool applies rotation and flipping together with an instant live preview, no quality loss.

Features

  • Rotate 90°, 180°, or 270° with one tap each
  • Flip horizontal and flip vertical, independently or combined
  • Live preview updates instantly
  • Reset button to start over

How to Use

Upload an image, then tap Rotate 90°/180°/270° to turn it, or Flip horizontal/vertical to mirror it. The preview updates live — download once it looks right, or tap Reset to start over.

Benefits

  • No resampling means zero quality loss from rotating or flipping
  • Combine both operations in a single pass before downloading
  • Fully touch-friendly on mobile

Rotate versus flip

Rotating turns the whole picture around its centre in 90° steps; a phone photo that opens sideways usually needs a 90° or 270° turn. Flipping creates a mirror image, horizontally (left becomes right) or vertically (top becomes bottom). The two can be combined in one pass, so you can turn a picture and mirror it before downloading.

When you need each one

  • Rotate 90°/270°: fix sideways photos and scans taken in the wrong orientation.
  • Rotate 180°: correct pictures that came out upside down.
  • Flip horizontal: correct mirrored selfies, or mirror a design for printing on transfers and some products.
  • Flip vertical: create reflections or fix inverted scans.

Note that flipping text produces reversed letters, so avoid it on images where text must stay readable.

Quality and next steps

Turning in right angles and mirroring only rearrange existing pixels, so the picture is not resampled and does not lose sharpness. If the horizon is slightly tilted rather than turned by a full right angle, crop the image after rotating with Image Crop, which also offers left and right rotation. You can reduce the file with Compress Image, change format with the Image Format Converter, or assemble several corrected pictures into one document using Image to PDF. For a different background behind a subject, see the Background Changer. The tool is touch-friendly and works entirely in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I combine rotate and flip?
Yes — rotation and flipping apply together, so you can straighten a sideways photo and mirror it in the same pass.

Does this reduce image quality?
No — rotating and flipping don't resample pixels the way resizing does, so quality is preserved.
