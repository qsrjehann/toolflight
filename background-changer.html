About the Background Changer

Already have a transparent-background image — from the AI Background Remover or elsewhere? Background Changer places it on a new solid color, gradient, or custom background image in seconds.

Features

  • 6 preset colors plus a full custom color picker
  • Adjustable linear gradient with angle control
  • Upload any image as a custom background
  • Direct handoff from the AI Background Remover

How to Use

Upload a transparent-background image, choose Solid color, Gradient, or Custom image, then adjust and download. Works seamlessly with the AI Background Remover's output.

Benefits

  • Full-resolution output matching your uploaded transparent image
  • No account, no per-image cost
  • Works entirely offline in your browser

Three ways to restyle a cut-out

  • Solid colour: pick from six presets or any custom colour, ideal for white product shots and brand-coloured profile pictures.
  • Gradient: blend two colours with an adjustable angle for banners and social graphics.
  • Custom image: place your subject on a scene, texture or branded backdrop.

For the best result, start from a clean transparent PNG. If your picture still has its original background, run it through the AI Background Remover first, which can hand the result over directly.

Good backgrounds for common uses

Marketplaces usually prefer plain white or light grey backgrounds for products. Profile pictures work well on a solid brand colour that contrasts with hair and clothing. For banners, a subtle gradient adds depth without distracting from the subject. When using a photo as a backdrop, choose one that is slightly blurred or low in contrast so the subject stands out, and match the lighting direction where possible so the composite looks natural.

After you change the background

The output keeps the resolution of your transparent image, so it is ready for print or web. If you plan to upload it to a site with a size limit, reduce it using Compress Image, and use PNG when you want to keep edges crisp. For official ID photos, follow the rules in our guide to passport photo requirements and use the Passport Photo Maker. Sellers can finish product listings with the Ecommerce Product Editor, and you can protect artwork with Image Watermark. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my image need transparency first?
This tool composites your subject onto a new background — it needs to know which pixels are the subject versus the old background, which is exactly what a transparent PNG/WEBP encodes.

Is the result full resolution?
Yes — the download matches the resolution of the transparent image you uploaded.
